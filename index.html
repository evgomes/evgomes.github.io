About

I am an entrepreneur, software architect, and applied AI specialist focused on turning complex processes into reliable systems, automation workflows, and practical AI solutions.

Over the years, I have worked across government, healthcare, education, real estate, and international consulting projects. That background taught me to think beyond prototypes and focus on operations, reliability, and real business constraints.

Today, my work sits at the intersection of software architecture, intelligent automation, and AI adoption. I design and build solutions that connect AI to real workflows, documents, APIs, internal systems, and human decision-making. I do this as Founding Software Engineer at BusyKeys and founder of Fronteira Labs.

My focus is not limited to building systems. I also help teams understand where AI creates value, where human oversight is necessary, and how to adopt the technology without creating fragile processes.
